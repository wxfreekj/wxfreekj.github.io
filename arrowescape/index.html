Tap an arrow. Its head travels straight out of the board and the body follows its original bends. A blocked exit costs a life. Clear all arrows to earn two arrow hints. Grid hints start at three; earn one additional grid hint for every five new campaign levels completed. Tap # to reveal faint lines aligned with all remaining arrowheads, including diagonals.
