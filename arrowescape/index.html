How to play & game rules

Tap directly on an arrow and release. Ambiguous touches do not cost lives. Pinch outward if arrows are too close to select confidently. The arrowhead travels straight out of the board and the body follows its original bends. The first blocked attempt on an arrow costs one life and marks it red until it escapes. Repeated touches on that arrow cost no additional lives. Resetting the level clears red marks. Arrow and grid hints each have a maximum of five. Complete campaign levels 5, 10, 15 and so on for the first time to earn a grid hint; every 10th level also earns an arrow hint. Out of arrow hints? Watch a short video for one more (a few per day). Regular puzzle timers pause in settings or when the app is hidden. Timed competitions do not pause. Best times are stored for each exact puzzle. Tap # to reveal faint lines aligned with all remaining arrowheads, including diagonals. Toggle it off and on free for the same attempt. After 30 seconds without a successful move, the available-move count appears automatically. Pinch inward to shrink the entire board to 35% of its fitted size, pinch outward to enlarge, or drag to pan. Fit restores the default view. Enable unlimited hints in Settings; existing finite credits are preserved. Puzzle geometry applies to new or reset levels. Optional practice levels are available from How to play. Real campaigns have no introductory levels or ending. Timed competition counts fully cleared puzzles, runs continuously, and disables both hints. Daily puzzles use the UTC date.
