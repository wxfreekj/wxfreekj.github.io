Play your way

Same seed, difficulty and level count = identical campaign. Each level becomes progressively harder.

Tap an arrow. Its head travels straight out of the board and the body follows its original bends. A blocked exit costs a life. Arrow and grid hints each have a maximum of five. Complete campaign levels 5, 10, 15 and so on for the first time to earn one of each. Your timer pauses in settings or when the app is hidden. Best times are stored for each exact puzzle. Tap # to reveal faint lines aligned with all remaining arrowheads, including diagonals. Toggle it off and on free for the same attempt. After 30 seconds without a successful move, the available-move count appears automatically. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, or use the zoom controls. Daily puzzles use the UTC date.
