Play your way

Tap an arrow. Its head travels straight out of the board and the body follows its original bends. A blocked exit costs a life. Clear all arrows to earn two arrow hints. Grid hints start at three; earn one additional grid hint for every five new campaign levels completed. Tap # to reveal faint lines aligned with all remaining arrowheads, including diagonals. Toggle it off and on free for the same attempt. After 30 seconds without a successful move, the available-move count appears automatically. Pinch to zoom, drag to pan, or use the zoom controls. Daily puzzles use the UTC date.
