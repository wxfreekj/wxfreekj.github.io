A DIFFERENT WAY TO DROP

Make room in 3D.

01

Find your space.

Move your piece left, right, forward and backward through a 5 × 5 well. Drag the well to look around; the floor’s X and Z arrows keep you oriented.

02

Turn things around.

Select an axis and turn in either direction. X and Z tip your piece; Y spins it flat. Lower a piece a little if the ceiling prevents a turn.

03

Clear an entire layer.

Fill all 25 spaces in a horizontal layer to clear it. The layers above move down. The ghost and top-down map show where your piece will land.

You can hold one piece each turn. Soft drop lowers it one cell; Drop sends it straight to its landing position. The game ends when a new piece has no room to appear.

Clear five layers to level up. Layers score 100 / 300 / 500 / 800 points for one / two / three / four at once, multiplied by your level. Soft drops earn 1 point per cell; hard drops earn 2.

Keyboard: arrows move; Q/E turn X, A/D turn Y, Z/C turn Z. Space drops, Shift holds, Enter starts or resumes, P pauses. Hold B to soft-drop.

Leaving the page pauses the game. Saved games always reopen paused. Camera movement never moves a piece.
